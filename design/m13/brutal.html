Build notes

  • Update brutal task row/tree rendering in tasks/task_tile.dart and shared task list grouping in tasks/task_list_page.dart; reuse the same tree rows in the month agenda and week day columns.
  • Tree line: light BrutalTheme.light.cobalt #3355FF, dark #98ACFF (dark primary); 2 px line, short branches, smaller quiet card rows. Done child uses mint checkbox and muted strike.
  • Skipped rows use neutral hairline #E3D9C6 tag/checkbox, muted title with dashed strike and ⊘ marker; completed rows keep mint check and solid strike.
  • Skip lives beside Done/Delete in tasks/task_detail.dart as a tomato action; after skipping show the existing ink snackbar treatment with yellow outline and Undo.
  • Missed repeats preserve the overdue occurrence and add the same title in Today as its next occurrence. Settings adds a Tasks section choice, stored per device; default is “Open details and show subtasks”.
  • Files/widgets: task_tile.dart, task_detail.dart, task_list_page.dart, calendar/month_view.dart, calendar/week_view.dart, settings/settings_page.dart; shared subtask row/tree widget and task tap preference.
